Our mission

Digital skills for every learner in Basse.

Our mission is to improve digital skills and computer literacy in Basse and the surrounding communities, so that more young people can learn, earn and create with technology.

  • No experience needed. We start from the basics.
  • Hands-on practice, not just theory.
  • Taught locally, by someone from the community.

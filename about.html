About us

Building digital skills, one learner at a time.

Code with Jallow is an ICT training initiative under JTECH Academy, based in Basse, Upper River Region.

Our background

Why Code with Jallow exists

Technology is changing how we learn, work and run businesses, but many young people in Basse and nearby communities still have limited access to computer training.

Code with Jallow was started to close that gap. Under JTECH Academy, we teach practical ICT skills, from basic computer use and typing to programming and web development, and we take digital literacy beyond the classroom through workshops and outreach.

We are committed to improving digital literacy and programming skills among students, youths and other community members.

Our mission

To improve digital skills and computer literacy in Basse and the surrounding communities through affordable, practical and beginner-friendly training.

Our vision

A digitally confident Upper River Region where every young person can use technology to learn, earn and build a better future.

Our objectives

What we are working towards

  • Teach basic computer skills to students, youths and adults.
  • Build programming and web development skills for the next generation of developers.
  • Introduce networking and robotics as resources become available.
  • Run community digital-literacy programmes, workshops and outreach.
  • Showcase and celebrate student projects and achievements.
In our community

Activities in Basse and beyond

We run regular training sessions in Basse and plan outreach in surrounding communities, including digital-literacy sessions, school workshops and student showcases.

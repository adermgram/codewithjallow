          Questions
          
    Good to know
    
          
    Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and we will reply.
    
        
    
        
    
          
    Do I need any experience?
    No. Our beginner courses start from the very basics, and you can move on to programming and web development when you are ready.
    
          
    Who can join?
    Students, youths and adults from Basse and the surrounding communities. Fill in the short registration form to get started.
    
          
    Are classes in-person or online?
    Classes are mainly in-person in Basse. Some courses, such as Programming and Web Development, also offer an online option. Each course page shows its learning mode.
    
          
    How much do courses cost?
    Each course page has a fee section. Fees are being finalised, so contact us to confirm the current price before you register.
    
          
    What happens after I register?
    You will see a confirmation message straight away, and we will contact you by phone to confirm your place, schedule and fee.
